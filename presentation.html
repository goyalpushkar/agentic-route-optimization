2. Development Methodology & Core Solvers

Mathematical Formulation: Multi-Compartment VRP (MC-VRPTW)

Strict priority hierarchy balancing customer service, driver safety, and operational spend

Objective Function & Penalties
Min Z = w1·Cost(OTIF) + w2·Cost(Overtime) + w3·Cost(Miles) + w4·Cost(Fleet)
• Priority Hierarchy: w1 >> w2 >> w3 >> w4 (On-time retail satisfaction prioritized first).
• Soft Windows (±1hr): Graduated penalty slopes outside the 1-hour buffer; 30 min standard stop dwell time.
• LIFO Constraint: Stop sequence matches reverse pallet loading so later drops never block early drops.
Dual Operational Engine Modes
• Mode A (Daily Dispatch): Runs nightly on firm customer orders; outputs turn-by-turn routes, bulkhead positions, and carrier bookings.
• Mode B (2-Month Forecast Simulator): Monte Carlo capacity simulator running over 2-month probabilistic demand to forecast trailer fleet adequacy.
• 3-Month Backtesting Engine: Re-runs historical order streams to benchmark empirical cost and mileage savings over legacy human dispatch.
